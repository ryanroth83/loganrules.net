3D Printing

Welcome to the 3D Printing section! Here you'll find projects, tips, and resources related to 3D printing.

My 3D Printer

Ender 3 v2: My main printer for all my projects. Reliable, precise, and easy to use.

My 3D Printing Settings

  • Print Temperature: 210°C for PLA+, 200–205°C for Silk PLA (glossy finish)
  • Bed Temperature: 60°C for PLA+ and Silk PLA
  • Print Speed: 50mm/s normally, 30mm/s for detailed models, up to 75mm/s for quick prints
  • Supports: Tree supports for overhangs and complex geometries
  • Infill: 20% for most prints, 30%+ for functional parts
  • Cooling: Enabled for PLA+ and Silk PLA; fan speed 100% after first few layers

My 3D Printing Software

I use Cura for slicing, MakerLab for custom designs, Tinkercad for beginner-friendly modeling, Printables for downloading community models, and OctoPrint to control prints remotely via OctoEverywhere.
